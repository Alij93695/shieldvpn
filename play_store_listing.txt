App Name: ShieldVPN - VPN Client

Short Description:
Client for the volunteer-run VPNGate network. One tap, servers worldwide.

Full Description:
ShieldVPN is a free, no-account VPN client for Android. It connects you to the public VPNGate network — thousands of volunteer-run servers around the world, published by an academic project at the University of Tsukuba, Japan.

Tap once. ShieldVPN times a sample of well-rated servers from your own phone, skips the ones that do not answer, and connects you to the fastest — falling back automatically if a community server is busy or offline.

Key Features:
- One-tap connect, no sign-up, no account, no subscription
- Thousands of servers across about 70 countries
- Automatic mode times servers from your phone and picks the fastest
- Servers that do not answer from your network are skipped, not dialled
- Automatic fallback: if a free server is overloaded, the app tries the next best one
- Standard OpenVPN protocol with an encrypted tunnel
- DNS routed inside the tunnel to prevent DNS leaks
- IPv6 leak protection
- No ads, no trackers, no analytics. The app contacts only the server-list mirror (with vpngate.net as a fallback) and the VPN servers themselves.

PLEASE READ BEFORE INSTALLING

ShieldVPN does not operate its own VPN servers. It is a client for the free, public VPNGate network. Your traffic is carried by volunteer operators who we do not control or audit.

The server directory is loaded from a public community mirror on GitHub, which also works on networks where vpngate.net itself is blocked.

The VPNGate project keeps connection logs and may disclose them where legally required, and individual volunteer operators may keep their own logs. ShieldVPN therefore cannot offer a "no-logs" guarantee, and we will not pretend otherwise.

Use ShieldVPN to reach a network from a different location and to protect traffic on untrusted Wi-Fi. Always use HTTPS. Do not use it for traffic where interception by an unknown third party would put you at risk.

Free community servers vary in speed and availability. They are shared by many people and performance is not guaranteed.
